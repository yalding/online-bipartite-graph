progress beyond the previously known $1.753$ lower bound for the general
vertex-arrival model.  The authors then suggested that the recent blueprint
framework of Assadi, Jiang, and Xiang might apply.  Prompted by this suggestion,

lower bound proved in this paper and assisted with drafting the manuscript.

exposition and can directly defend every aspect of the submission.  The authors
